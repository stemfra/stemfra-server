- "If we don't bring you paying clients, you owe us nothing" / "performance fee": FALSE. We charge

  the venue on the owner's own terminal; we add no payment fee.
- "AI receptionist answers the phone / books a $300 plumbing job": the Front Desk is a CHAT
  widget on the tenant site, books free services in chat and hands priced ones to the booking
  page; there is no tenant voice agent. Plumbers, roofers and medspas are not our verticals.
- "No human in the loop": the wizard is self-serve, but the AI finish job, Stacy and a human on
  request all exist, and the sales motion is calls. Keep offering the human.
- Unicorn talk: irrelevant with zero clients. The only question is the first ten.

**Right, and already ours:**
- The $8,000 crossover (5% = $400 = the cap): the point where a mainstream tool is $250 all-in.
  Our answer is the list of what only we include (Pricing comparison §2). Reps must know it.
- "Interactive preview in 60 seconds from Google data" = P27 claim prefill + the frictionless
  claim flow, the prerequisite for the ad pilot. Endorsed; already on the roadmap.
- The "24/7 front desk on commission, capped at $400" frame works for CHAT, if we say chat.

**New and worth doing:**
- **Auto-confirm anxiety.** Owners who forget to mark a no-show get billed 5% on a visit that never
  happened and must dispute it afterwards. Fix in product, not copy: a pre-invoice review
  email/bell 3 days before the statement ("these N bookings will be billed; anything that did
  not happen? mark it") plus the same list on the dashboard. Recorded as ROADMAP P43.
- **Organic TikTok/Instagram outreach** (DMs and comments to owners posting "DM to book") is
  cheap and matches the pool (Chrystal's Beauty: FB 380, IG 670, TikTok 300 followers, no
  site). Do it by hand, low volume, never scraped (TikTok/Meta terms); paid ads stay Meta first
  (TikTok has no owner targeting; B2B CPL $20 to $80, "brutal in the bottom quartile").

Why TikTok Changes the Game for Stemfra
In the beauty and wellness industries, there has been a massive structural shift away from traditional, large employee-based salons toward booth renters, suite owners, and independent solo artists.
The Reality: These younger entrepreneurs live entirely on TikTok. They use it as their primary portfolio to get clients.
The Match: A solo lash tech or barber making $4,000 a month doesn't want to pay $150/month for a legacy booking software. Stemfra’s 5% commission with a no-human-in-the-loop setup is a perfect match for them. They are easily found and pitched directly on TikTok.
Meta ads are great for static images and text, but Stemfra's biggest selling point is a live, data-driven AI that interacts with clients.
The Power of TikTok: TikTok is built for fast-paced, screen-recording style videos. A TikTok showing a split-screen—on the left, a user typing a chaotic text command ("Hey, can I book a skin fade and a beard trim for Thursday afternoon if you have a spot after 3?"), and on the right, Stemfra's AI cleanly checking the live staff info and booking it in 2 seconds—goes viral incredibly easily. It proves the technology works instantly in a format younger owners trust.
Local beauty and wellness businesses on TikTok are constantly begging for clients in their comment sections.
The Hustle: Stemfra's growth team can use TikTok to find salon videos where customers are commenting: "Where can I book?" or "Are you open this Saturday?"
The Entryway: If the salon owner replies, "DM me on Instagram to book!" that is a broken funnel. Stemfra can step in organically and say: "You are losing money forcing clients to jump platforms. Put a Stemfra link in your bio, let the AI book them automatically, and pay $0 upfront." Meta doesn't offer this kind of public, intent-driven organic hunting ground.
The Winning Play: A Two-Pronged Funnel
The smartest move for Stemfra is to use both platforms for what they do best:
Use TikTok for Aggressive, Low-Cost Top-of-Funnel Volume: Target younger solo-preneurs, barbers, and creators with organic outreach and visual trend ads showcasing the 60-second wizard onboarding.
Use Meta for High-Value, Mid-Market Retargeting: Use Meta Ads to target larger, established spas, CrossFit boxes, and multi-location salons. Frame the copy around the $400 commission cap to prove how much money they will save compared to legacy SaaS or uncapped transaction models.
