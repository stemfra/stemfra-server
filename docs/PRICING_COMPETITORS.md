## The read on 10% (rejected 2026-09-15)

- A flat 10% on all bookings is not defensible against this field. The mainstream tools land
  at 2.7% to 3.5% all-in for an established shop, so 5% is already the ceiling that only
  Mindbody, Mangomint, Boulevard and WellnessLiving reach, and they bring deeper software. At
  10% a shop pays $500 a month more than Fresha or Booksy, and a rep cannot cover that with
  "no monthly fee".
- Nobody charges 10% on repeat business. The high rates (Fresha 20%, Booksy 30%, StyleSeat
  30%, Mindbody 20%) are one-time fees on the first visit of a client the platform brought
  in. Their marketing message is "returning clients are free". A 10% on the shop's own
  regulars is the exact thing they advertise against.
- Where 10% works: as a performance fee on bookings Stemfra generates. 10% on new clients
  from the marketplace, the AI Front desk, win-back and review campaigns is far below
  Fresha's 20% and easy to sell. A two-rate model, 5% on everything and 10% on
  Stemfra-sourced or recovered bookings, is the version the market supports.
- The small shop risk is the sharpest. A solo barber doing $3k a month pays $150 at 5%,
  already above Booksy at about $110 or Square free at about $80. At 10% that becomes $300,
  more than Mangomint charges with far more features, and Square and Setmore are genuinely
  free.
- Fresha ending its free plan helps the current message. "Free website and booking, no
  monthly fee" is now rare, and a commission rise weakens the one line that separates
  Stemfra from Squarespace and Wix.

Unverified numbers (JavaScript-rendered or quote-only vendor pages): Square Appointments paid
tiers, Squarespace plan prices, Mindbody upper tiers and processing, Momence, Zenoti,
Boulevard's standard processing rate.

## Open discussion (from the ROADMAP block)

How the 5% is justified by what is delivered, which is not a generic website: an agency
charges thousands to build the same site, and the offer adds booking, reminders, reviews,
win-back, memberships, the marketplace listing and the AI Front desk. Write that argument for
the pricing page and the sales script, and consider the performance fee (higher only on
Stemfra-sourced bookings) as the growth lever instead of a higher flat rate.
