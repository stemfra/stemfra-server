_For Peter to paste into Claude Design to generate Facebook page posts and TikTok clips for the
six verticals. Every prompt below starts with the same brand brief so the outputs match the
site. Rules baked in: no em-dash anywhere, "Stemfra" in sentence case, the price as it reads on

Companion: `AD_FUNNEL_2026-09.md` (the paid-ad version and the Instant Form)._

## 0. Brand brief (paste at the top of EVERY prompt)

```
You are designing social media posts for Stemfra (stemfra.com), a company that builds and runs
websites with online booking for barbershops, beauty salons, massage studios, day spas, yoga and
Pilates studios and CrossFit boxes in the US, Canada and the UK. The owner gets a website of
their own, on their own domain, built for them, with booking, reminders, client win-back and
Google review requests. Clients see only that business, never a marketplace with other shops
beside it. Price: free to claim, no monthly plan, a flat 5% of what is booked through the site,
never more than $400 a month. Clients pay the business at the venue; Stemfra adds no payment fee.

Voice: calm, plain, confident, no hype, no exclamation marks, no emojis in headlines. Short
sentences. Write "Stemfra" in sentence case, never "STEMfra" or "STEMFRA". Never use an em-dash
(use a colon, a comma or a full stop). Never say "pay on success", "we only earn if we bring you
clients", "AI receptionist that answers your phone", or quote a testimonial, a client count or a
result we have not stated here.

Look: the stemfra.com register. Palette: ink #2B2622 (dark surface), cream #E6DDCD (text on
ink), paper #F5EFE6 (light surface), gold #C9B891 (one rare accent, hairlines and eyebrows).
Type: Hanken Grotesk (or Inter) for everything; big light-weight display headlines, small
tracked uppercase eyebrows. Square corners, hairline dividers, generous space, one photo or one
illustration per post, never stock-photo collages. Logo: the S mark in cream on ink, wordmark
"STEMFRA" tracked out beneath it, small, bottom corner.

CTA on every post: "Start free at stemfra.com". Never "Book a call".
```

## 2. "Still booking by DM?" animated clip (TikTok, 1080x1920, 15 seconds)

```
Create a 15-second vertical animation (1080x1920, 30 fps) for TikTok, silent-safe (every idea is
readable without sound), with a light click-track feel. No voice needed; I will add music.

Storyboard:
0 to 3 s: a phone screen mock-up on ink. An Instagram-style bio card for "Chrystal's Beauty" with
the line "DM to book" highlighted. Caption slides in: "Still booking by DM?"
3 to 7 s: the phone flips to a clean website for the same salon: name at the top, a Book button,
a service list with prices, a time picker. A finger taps Book, picks "Gel manicure, 2:30 pm",
a "Booked" tick appears. Caption: "Your own site. Your clients book themselves."
7 to 10 s: a chat bubble on the site: a client asks "Do you do lashes on Saturdays?" and the
site answers "Yes, 9 am to 4 pm. Want to book?" Caption: "A front desk that answers while you
work." (it is a chat, never show a phone call)
10 to 13 s: the price, large, on ink: "Free to claim. 5% when it books. Never more than $400 a
month." Caption below: "No monthly plan."
13 to 15 s: the S mark, "STEMFRA", and "Start free at stemfra.com".

Style: the stemfra.com register (ink, cream, gold hairlines, Hanken Grotesk), motion by simple
slides and fades, no bouncing, no confetti. Export as MP4 (H.264) and also give me the frames as
PNG so I can use any single frame as a still post.
```

## 3. Carousel: "Marketplace vs your own site" (Facebook, 5 slides, 1080x1350)

```
Design a five-slide carousel for Facebook (1080x1350 each) that explains why a business should
own its booking page instead of renting one on a marketplace. Ink and paper alternate per slide.

Slide 1 (ink): headline "On a marketplace, your clients see every shop."
Slide 2 (paper): a simplified marketplace listing: your shop's card with three competitor cards
beside it, each with a "Book" button. Caption: "Every rebooking is a chance to look around."
Slide 3 (ink): the same shop as its own website: only its name, its photo, its Book button.
Caption: "On your own site, they see you."
Slide 4 (paper): three lines with gold hairlines between: "We build it for you." / "We move your
client list in." / "You swap the booking link on your Google profile."
Slide 5 (ink): "Free to claim. 5% when it books. Never more than $400 a month." and the CTA
"Start free at stemfra.com", logo bottom right.

Keep every slide readable at thumbnail size: one idea per slide, headline never under 64 px.
Export as five PNGs, numbered.
```
