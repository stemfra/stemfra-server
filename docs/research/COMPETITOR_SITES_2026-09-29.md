# Competitor sites closest to Stemfra: survey (2026-09-29)

_Asked by Peter after the feedback that stemfra.com "looked like a marketplace to find barbers".
Purpose: context for the home page decision, NOT a rebuild. Method: the web-clone skill's survey
phase, cut down to what the decision needs. No decision is taken here._

## Method and honest counts

- First pass by fetch over ten candidates (Squarespace, Acuity, GlossGenius, Fresha, Vagaro, Booksy,
  Square Appointments, Wix Bookings, Mindbody, Durable, B12). Wix's booking page returned 404, Durable
  redirected and was not followed, B12 returned its headline only, Square's hero was truncated.
- Five opened in Claude browser tabs for Peter and navigated: Squarespace (templates), GlossGenius
  (home, website builder), Booksy Biz (home, AI Receptionist), Mindbody (home, Customized Websites),
  Vagaro (home, MySite). Non-essential cookies declined on each.
- Screenshots: 10 taken, 9 usable (GlossGenius's home came back blank: scroll-reveal). Headings and
  navigation read from the DOM, page copy from the page text.
- NOT reached: mobile and tablet views, hover menus, a template detail page, any signed-in screen,
  design tokens. Prices came from the locale the network served (EUR for Squarespace and Mindbody,
  GBP for Vagaro, USD for Booksy and GlossGenius); US prices differ.

**Reading.** "Closest" depends on the axis, and three companies each win one:

- **Product shape and verticals: Vagaro.** Same pillars, same parts, same buyer.
- **The done-for-you promise: Mindbody.** "We build your website… No DIY required."
- **Positioning without a marketplace: GlossGenius.** Brand-first, agents in the top navigation.

Where Stemfra differs from Vagaro, its closest match: Vagaro runs a consumer marketplace beside the
owner's site (the thing our second section argues against), charges per bookable calendar per month
with paid add-ons, and its receptionist is chat only. Stemfra has no marketplace, no monthly fee
(5% of bookings, capped at $400), chat AND voice, and setup by a real team as the default.

Nobody else combines all four of: own website, booking, AI receptionist, done for you,
WITHOUT a marketplace and WITHOUT a monthly fee. Mindbody has the four with a fee and a marketplace;
Vagaro has them with a fee, add-ons and a marketplace; GlossGenius has three of four with a fee. The AI receptionist is now table stakes in this category
(Booksy, GlossGenius, Mindbody and Vagaro all name one), and three of them use the word
"Receptionist" or "Reception", which supports Peter's choice of that word for the hero.

## How they avoid looking like a marketplace

The feedback's claim was that Stemfra's theme section reads as a directory. What the others do:

| Site | How templates or sample sites are shown | Names | Card label | Frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squarespace /templates | H1 "Make any website template yours with ease.", H2 "Popular Designs Website Templates (29)", filters by Type (Online Store, Portfolio, Scheduling…) and Topic (Health & Beauty, Local Business…) | Abstract: Altaloma, Myhra, Comet, Quinn, Selene | Template name under the image; actions on hover | Flat screenshots |
| Shopify themes | "Choose the right theme for your business" | Abstract: Dawn, Prestige, Impact | "View theme" | Flat screenshots |
| GlossGenius website builder | "Build a site that turns clicks into clients", a carousel of sample sites | Sample business names inside the screenshots | None: the carousel is illustration, not a catalogue | Rounded browser-like frames, desktop + phone |
| Vagaro MySite | "Build a professional website that runs itself", eyebrow "WEBSITE BUILDER FOR SALONS, SPAS, AND FITNESS STUDIOS" | Sample business inside the mockup | None | Laptop + phone device frames |
| Mindbody Customized Websites | "We build your website…" | A sample site in a framed panel | None | Framed panels |
| **Stemfra home** | Heading "Designed for your business.", six tabs, then all six groups stacked: 16 cards | **Realistic business names** (Argyle & Sons, Ironclad Athletics) as the card title | "Preview" twice + "Start" | Flat |

Three patterns, all cheaper than a restructure:

1. **The word "template", "theme" or "website" sits in the heading** above any grid of sample sites.
   Every site above does this; ours says "Designed for your business." and "Barbershop".
2. **A catalogue uses abstract names; an illustration uses business names but no catalogue.**
   Squarespace and Shopify name templates like products. GlossGenius, Vagaro and Mindbody show
   business names only inside a framed screenshot that is clearly a picture of a website. Stemfra
   is the only one that lists business names as catalogue titles.
3. **Frames are used by the three closest competitors**, not by the two template stores. Frames are
   one way to signal "this is a website"; headings and labels are the other.

## Other observations

- **Home page anatomy of the closest three** (GlossGenius, Booksy, Mindbody): hero naming the
  audience or the outcome → product screens → numbers and customer stories → feature groups →
  "switching is easy, we do it" → industries → FAQ. All three SHOW the product (calendar, phone,
  chat) within the first two screens. Stemfra's home shows no product screen.
- **Audience in the hero.** Booksy names barbers, stylists and salon owners; Vagaro names beauty,
  wellness and fitness; Mindbody and GlossGenius name the outcome and list industries lower down.
  None says "for your business" alone.
- **Switching help** is on every closest competitor's home ("Let us handle switching for you.
  Free."). Stemfra has it in How it works, step 2.
- **Squarespace's hero** is "A website makes it real" + "Start for free. No credit card required.";
  ours is modelled on it, by Peter's decision of 2026-09-21.
- **Cookie consent.** All five sites open with a consent banner offering a reject option. Stemfra
  has none. Checked 2026-09-29: the marketing site loads no analytics or advertising script today,
  and the tenant sites write one localStorage entry for the boot loader (functional). A banner
  becomes necessary the day a Meta or TikTok pixel or any analytics cookie goes live, which is
  planned. Recorded as ROADMAP P46.

## What this does and does not show

It shows how the category presents itself and where Stemfra's offer is distinct. It does not show
which wording converts better for us: that needs our own visitors (a five-second test with five
people, then analytics once there is traffic).
