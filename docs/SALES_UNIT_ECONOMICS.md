## Website planning assumptions

USD299 monthly revenue; USD50 delivery allowance including AI/SMS/email/domain/payment fees; USD30 support allocation; USD350 fixed monthly Raiser base. Shared company subscriptions, initial onboarding labour, tools/advertising, other salaries and taxes are excluded. Allowances are not metered actual costs or enforced limits. Avoid double-counting support if its staffing cost is also included elsewhere.

Illustrative delivery split: AI18, SMS10, email1, domain2.50 (first-year USD30 allowance amortized), payments15, contingency3.50 = USD50. Domain renewal policy is not established by this assumption.

At 20% combined commission, monthly contribution per customer before the fixed base = 299 - 80 - 59.80 = USD159.20.

Three websites cover these modelled costs; twelve achieve at least USD100 contribution per customer after allocating the base. This assumes recurring 20% commission for every included customer/payment; a limited acquisition commission changes later months.

Formula: contribution = customers * (price * (1 - combined commission rate) - delivery - support) - fixed base.
Price floor for a target contribution per customer = (delivery + support + fixed base/customers + target contribution)/(1 - combined commission rate). Payment allowance must be revisited when price/payment method changes.

A USD50 fixed commission model discussed earlier is a separate scenario, not an additional cost on top of percentage commission. The earlier 25% income-tax reserve was illustrative only, not Stemfra's actual tax rate. Actual tax needs LLC classification and owner/jurisdiction review. Customer VAT/sales tax is separate from revenue. AI subscription costs need their own model.
